# West Michigan: managing bodies still to check

Generated 2026-11-13 from the research files in this folder. Each entry is a city, township, village, county or organisation whose hunting rule for its parks could not be read automatically, usually because its ordinance site refuses automated requests.

**How to use it.** Open the body's ordinance or park-rules page in your own browser, find the section on hunting, trapping, firearms or weapons in parks, and either paste the text into the Claude session or save the page as a PDF into `docs/research/inbox/` named after the body (for example `ada-township.pdf`). One rule usually covers all of that body's parks.

**What to look for.** A sentence such as "No person shall hunt, trap or discharge a firearm in any park", any exception (a named park, a permit, a managed deer hunt), and the date of the ordinance.

Until checked, these parks keep the default colour: red inside city limits, yellow in townships and villages. School-run land is already red.
