# Park hunting research

Research run 2026-11-12: for each park unit or park system, is any kind of hunting actually allowed? This is separate from the map's data: it records what published rules say, with sources, so the map can be compared against it.

**How far to trust it.** Only michigan.gov pages could be opened in full (including the Wildlife Conservation Order Chapter VII as amended through 2026-09-09). National Park Service, Fish and Wildlife Service, university and most county sites were unreachable, so those entries rest on search-result summaries. County results are thin: 20 of 83 counties have a finding. Confidence is given per row. Rules change; confirm with the managing agency before hunting.

### School property

All school-district property is graded closed (red), on the owner's instruction: Michigan law makes school property a weapon-free school zone (MCL 750.237a) and districts do not open land to public hunting. This applies to parcels whose owner name is a school district and to park features managed by a school. Universities and colleges are not included; they keep their own grading.
