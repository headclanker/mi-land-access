# Browser pass (2026-11-13)

Ordinance text read page by page in an ordinary browser (ecode360 / Municode / American Legal pages a person would open), plus the bodies' own PDFs. Findings, one per managing body:

## Grand Haven Charter Township — red (high)
- Park Rules and Regulations Ordinance (Ord. 59, as amended by Ord. 536, 2015), Sec. 4: no person shall "Hunt or trap any animals or birds within any portion of a Township Park or recreation area", nor possess a firearm or weapon (bows included) there except police / CPL holders.
- Source: https://ghtmi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/20.0300-Ordinance-59-Park-Rules-Regulations-Ordinance.pdf
- Covers: Hofma Park & Preserve, Pottawattomie Park, Mercury Park, Odawa/Battle Point Launch, Millhouse Park.

## Township of Cannon — still open (rule covers an unmapped park only)
- Ch. 313 Art. I applies to Bostwick Lake Park only ("PARK: Bostwick Lake Park…"); bans hunting, trapping, bows and firearm discharge there. Nothing found for Cannon Township Center Property.
- Source: https://ecode360.com/34321547

## Park Township — still open (zone rule only)
- No park rule. Ch. 22 Art. VI Div. 2, Sec. 22-155 (MAC R 317.170.1): hunting with firearm, air gun or bow prohibited in the E 1/2 of the SE 1/4 of Section 22 and everywhere south of Ottawa Beach Road (waterfowl on Lake Macatawa excepted). Sec. 22-156: Pine Creek Bay waters. Ch. 28 Art. III (parks) covers alcohol and litter only.
- Source: https://ecode360.com/PA3519 (search "hunt")
- Earlier research put Winstrom, Cooper-VanWieren, Stu Visser, Wendt and Ransom north of the line, so they stay yellow.

## Allendale Charter Township — red (high)
- Code Ch. 300 Parks and Recreation Areas, § 300-3(D) Hunting: "No person shall hunt, trap, catch, wound, kill, or treat cruelly, or attempt to … any bird or animal." (The separate firearms subsection was repealed in 2012.)
- Source: https://ecode360.com/38007559
- Covers: Allendale Community Park.

## Port Sheldon Township — "Port Sheldon Park" yellow, archery only (medium)
- Hunting and Target Practice Policy (Nov 2025), table: 4. Port Sheldon Street (Township Hall and Fire Station, 70-11-22-400-006): firearm hunting Not Allowed, archery hunting Allowed, no target practice. 5. Windsnest Park, 6. Sheldon Landing/Crab Drive, 7. Kouw Park: nothing allowed. 1–3 (Stanton, Hiawatha, Van Buren Street parcels): firearm and archery allowed.
- The PAD-US "Port Sheldon Park" (12 ac, 42.886 N, 86.183 W) sits at Port Sheldon Street and 160th Avenue, the Township Hall / rec-centre parcel on the policy map. Zwemer Park is not in the policy.
